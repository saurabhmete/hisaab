What layout does it expect?{{ icon('chevron-down', 18, 'chev') }}

Year sheets: one per year, named after the year (e.g. 2026)

  • Row 1: Total Salary in column A, then one salary figure per month.
  • Row 2: Expenses in column A, then month names (January, February, …). If the months wrap past December, the wrapped months go into the next year.
  • Following rows: one expense category per row: the name in column A, amounts under each month. Empty cells are skipped.
  • Optional: a Can Invest row sets the amount invested each month.
  • Rows named Total Expense, Salary Remaining… and Remainder… are ignored; the app works those out.

Investment sheet: any sheet with "Investment" in its name

  • Row 1: month names across the columns (assigned to the latest year sheet's year).
  • Labeled rows in column A: India Investment Value (Rupees), India Profit (Rupees), Germany Investment Value (Euros), Germany Profit (Euros) and EUR to INR rate.
  • Net worth and profit percentages are recomputed; those rows are ignored.

Bank or card statements, a spreadsheet laid out differently, another app's export, even notes: give them to Claude with the prompt below and it converts them into a file Hisaab can read. Got a loan? That has its own importer under Loans.

1

Copy the prompt

It already lists your current expense categories, so Claude reuses them instead of inventing near-duplicates.

2

Ask Claude

  1. Open a new chat in Claude and paste the prompt.
  2. Attach your records (PDFs, spreadsheets, CSV or screenshots).
  3. Claude will ask you a few questions first, such as what counts as salary or which payments are investments. Answer them; that's what makes the file accurate.
  4. Download the hisaab-data.json it gives you, or copy the JSON if it shows a code block.

The prompt tells Claude to leave your name, address and account numbers out of the file.
