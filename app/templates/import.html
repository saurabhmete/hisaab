A · I have the Salary Plan spreadsheet

Months that already exist are overwritten with the workbook's values. The layout it expects is described further down.

B · My records are in some other form

Bank or card statements, a spreadsheet laid out differently, another app's export, even notes: give them to Claude together with the prompt below and it converts them into a file Hisaab can read.

1 · Copy the prompt

It already lists your current expense categories, so Claude reuses them instead of inventing near-duplicates.

2 · Ask Claude

  1. Open a new chat in Claude and paste the prompt.
  2. Attach your records (PDFs, spreadsheets, CSV or screenshots).
  3. Claude will ask you a few questions first, such as what counts as salary or which payments are investments. Answer them; that's what makes the file accurate.
  4. Download the hisaab-data.json it gives you, or copy the JSON if it shows a code block.

The prompt tells Claude to leave your name, address and account numbers out of the file.

Expected Excel layout for option A

1 · Year sheets — one per year, named after the year (e.g. 2026)

  • Row 1: Total Salary in column A, then one salary figure per month.
  • Row 2: Expenses in column A, then month names (January, February, …) — one column per month. If the months wrap past December (…Nov, Dec, Jan), the wrapped months are placed in the next year.
  • Following rows: one expense category per row — the category name in column A, amounts under each month column. Empty cells are skipped.
  • Optional: a Can Invest row sets the amount invested that month.
  • Rows named Total Expense, Salary Remaining…, and Remainder… are ignored — the app computes those itself.

2 · Investment sheet — any sheet with "Investment" in its name

  • Row 1: month names across the columns (these months are assigned to the latest year sheet's year).
  • Labeled rows (matched by name, in column A): India Investment Value (Rupees), India Profit (Rupees), Germany Investment Value (Euros), Germany Profit (Euros), and EUR to INR rate.
  • Net worth and profit percentages are recomputed by the app — those rows are ignored.
